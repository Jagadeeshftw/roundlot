## On-chain fill: QuoterV2

`quote` and `plan_trade` price trades with Uniswap's QuoterV2 (`0xd1b797d92d87b688193a2b976efc8d577d204343`) against the wrapper's pool, whose address comes from the Uniswap v3 factory:

| Side and size unit | Quote |
|---|---|
| buy, `usd` | exact input: this much stablecoin in |
| buy, `shares` | exact output: this many shares out |
| sell, `shares` | exact input: this many shares in |
| sell, `usd` | exact output: this much stablecoin out |

The result gives the amounts, the **average price per share**, the **pool mid per share** and **price impact** (average versus mid, in basis points).

## Partial fills are refused

If a swap would push the price to the edge of the pool's range, QuoterV2 returns a partial fill instead of failing. Roundlot detects that (the post-swap price hits the tick bound, or the exact-output quote reverts) and refuses with `insufficient_liquidity`. You get an error and no charge, never a quote for less than you asked.

<Hint type="warning" title="Thin pools.">
TSLA's pool is the thinnest of the three: on 23 Sep 2026 a $10,000 buy moved it about 94 bps, against 17 bps for NVDA and 12 bps for SPY. Sizes that exhaust in-range liquidity are refused.
</Hint>

## Reference: the OKX spot book

Roundlot reads OKX's public order book and ticker for `XNVDA-USDT`, `XSPY-USDT` and `XTSLA-USDT`, trying `www.okx.com` and then regional hosts. The quote's `reference` block gives bid, ask, mid and spread, and `onchainEdgeBps`:

- for a buy, how much cheaper the on-chain average is than the OKX ask;
- for a sell, how much better it is than the OKX bid.

Positive means the on-chain fill beats crossing the OKX book. USDG and USDC are treated as equal to USDT.

## Freshness

| Data | Cached for |
|---|---|
| OKX order book and ticker | 2 s |
| Pool state (price, liquidity) | 2 s |
| Token state (multiplier, pause, wrapper rate) | 30 s |
| OKX instrument state | 60 s |
| Pool addresses | 24 h |

Every response carries the mainnet block number it was read at.
