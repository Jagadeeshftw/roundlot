## Do I need an OKX account?

No. Any wallet that can sign an EIP-712 message can pay. Testnet USD₮0 comes from the X Layer faucet. The [OKX Agentic Wallet](/docs/pay-agentic-wallet) is one convenient option.

## Why are payments on testnet?

So anyone can try every tool without spending real money. The data and the trade plans are real X Layer mainnet; mainnet payments are configured and switched off.

## Do I need gas?

No. The facilitator submits each payment and pays its gas.

## Does Roundlot hold keys or funds?

No. `plan_trade` returns unsigned transactions for your account to sign and send. Roundlot only receives the per-call fee. See [Security and trust](/docs/security).

## What if a call fails?

Payment settles only after the tool succeeds. A refused quote or plan, such as a size the pool can't fill, is never charged.

## Which MCP clients can pay?

Clients that follow the `@x402/mcp` convention or send x402 headers, such as OKX's CLI and Agent Payments skill. Clients that can't sign see the price and the REST alternative instead. See [x402 over MCP](/docs/paying-mcp).

## Why do on-chain and OKX prices differ?

They're different markets. The on-chain pool is a Uniswap v3 pool on X Layer; OKX is a centralized order book. Outside US hours OKX's price is an estimate. `quote` reports the gap as `onchainEdgeBps`.

## Why only NVDA, SPY and TSLA?

They're the xStocks with usable Uniswap v3 liquidity on X Layer today.
