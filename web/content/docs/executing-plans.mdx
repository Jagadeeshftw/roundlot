## The steps, in order

| Step | Contract | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `approve` | the token being spent | Lets the next step spend exactly the amount it needs. Skipped when the allowance is already enough |
| `wrap` | the ERC-4626 wrapper | `deposit`: turns raw xStock tokens into wrapped tokens. Only when selling the raw form |
| `swap` | SwapRouter02 `0x4f0c28f5926afda16bf2506d5d9e57ea190f9bca` | `multicall(deadline, [exactInputSingle or exactOutputSingle])` against the wrapper's Uniswap v3 pool |
| `unwrap` | the ERC-4626 wrapper | `redeem`: turns wrapped tokens into the raw xStock. Only when buying the raw form |

Each step depends on the one before it, so send them one at a time and wait for each receipt before sending the next.

## Slippage

`maxSlippageBps` (default 50, at most 1000) sets the swap's limit:

- **exact input** (buy in USD, sell in shares): `amountOutMinimum` is the quoted output less the slippage;
- **exact output** (buy in shares, sell in USD): `amountInMaximum` is the quoted input plus the slippage.

If the pool has moved further than that by the time the swap runs, the swap reverts and nothing is spent except gas.

## Deadlines

The swap is wrapped in `multicall(deadline, …)`, so it reverts after `deadlineSeconds` (default 600, from 60 to 3600). A plan past its deadline is stale: ask for a new one rather than sending it late.

## Approvals

Approvals are for the exact amount a step spends, never unlimited. Approvals the account already has are left out of the plan.

## Before you sign

<Hint type="warning" title="Check the targets.">
Compare every `to` address with [Supported assets and contracts](/docs/assets), and simulate each step (for example viem's `call` or `estimateGas`) before sending.
</Hint>

- Read `warnings`: a low balance means the plan will revert as-is.
- A multiplier change inside the deadline window makes `plan_trade` refuse with `multiplier_change_pending`; try again after it activates.
- Buying the raw form with exact input unwraps the slippage floor; any fill above it stays as wrapped tokens in the account.
