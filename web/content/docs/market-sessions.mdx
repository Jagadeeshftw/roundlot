## US equity session

`session` computes the NYSE session in New York time, including daylight saving:

| Phase | New York time |
|---|---|
| `pre_market` | 04:00 – 09:30 |
| `regular` | 09:30 – 16:00 |
| `post_market` | 16:00 – 20:00 |
| `closed` | overnight, weekends and NYSE holidays |

On early-close days regular trading ends at 13:00 and after-hours at 17:00. The calendar covers NYSE holidays and early closes for 2026 and 2027, from nyse.com.

Outside regular hours, OKX prices xStocks as the **last close plus a market estimate**, not a live underlying price, and `session` adds a warning saying so.

## What else is checked

- **OKX instrument state:** `live`, or another state such as `suspend`, with a warning when it isn't `live`.
- **Issuer pause:** a paused token makes `quote` and `plan_trade` refuse with `token_paused`.
- **Scheduled multiplier change:** reported with its activation time; `plan_trade` refuses when it falls inside the trade's deadline window.
- **Pool liquidity:** whether the pool has in-range liquidity, so `tradable` is `true` only when the token isn't paused and the pool can trade.
